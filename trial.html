      The corpus is empty
      
      
      Paste a text and press Add to corpus. The bot reads every word in its sentence, sets proper nouns aside, and files each form under its base word: jealousy under jealous, neighbours under neighbor.
      
      
      Every text you add goes into the same counts, and words the bot has already settled are reused, so later texts go faster.
